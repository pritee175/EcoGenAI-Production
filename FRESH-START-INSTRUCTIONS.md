## 🔄 Clear Your Browser (Important!)

To ensure a completely fresh start, clear your browser data:

### Option 1: Use Incognito/Private Mode (Easiest)
- **Chrome/Edge:** Press `Ctrl + Shift + N`
- **Firefox:** Press `Ctrl + Shift + P`
- Then go to: `http://localhost:3000`

### Option 2: Clear Browser Storage
1. Open: `http://localhost:3000`
2. Press `F12` (DevTools)
3. Go to **Console** tab
4. Type: `localStorage.clear()`
5. Press Enter
6. Refresh page (`F5`)
